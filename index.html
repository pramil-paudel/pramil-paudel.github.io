Bio

Researcher, engineer, and visual systems builder.

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Kansas working at the intersection of privacy-preserving machine learning and computational (lensless) imaging. My recent work explores how to train and deploy robust classifiers and image-to-image models (CNNs, GANs, Transformers) while minimizing exposure of sensitive visual data—e.g., steganographic encodings and defenses that keep signals useful for learning but opaque for humans. I care about methods that are measurable, reproducible, and practical to ship.

Before graduate school I spent ~5 years in industry as a software engineer and solution architect, building distributed systems with Java/Spring, Spark/Hadoop, Elasticsearch, Docker, and CI/CD on cloud. At KU I collaborate with physics on real-time, data-intensive workloads and have taught/assisted courses in Python, OOP, and Data Structures. I also serve in Nepali student and literature organizations. I'm open to collaborations on trustworthy AI, efficient training, and privacy-constrained imaging or healthcare ML.

Research Highlight

Seeing Without Revealing

From scene to encoded measurement to privacy analysis.

A research overview of the full lensless privacy pipeline.

1. Sensing: how ordinary scenes become lensless measurements.
2. Learning: classification or representation learning without direct reconstruction.
3. Hiding: lensless representation embedding inside natural RGB containers.
4. Attacking: reconstruction attempts and privacy leakage evaluation.
Research Systems

Research systems.

System 01 · Privacy & Security in Imaging

Direct learning from lensless measurements

Design privacy-preserving pipelines for lensless cameras that keep human-readable content obfuscated while retaining features needed for ML. Adversarially robust classification for degraded/encoded imagery with evaluation under common corruptions and threat models.

QuestionCan models learn semantic information from measurements that are not visually interpretable?
ApproachPSF simulation, lensless forward models, block-DCT features, CNN/ResNet classification.
OutputClassification experiments, frequency analysis, and privacy-oriented representation evaluation.
VenuesIEEE MASS 2024 · ICMLA 2025
Lensless Imaging Classification Frequency Features Visual Privacy Adversarial Robustness
System 02 · Representation Hiding

Privacy-preserving image-to-image steganography

Embedding full lensless representations inside natural RGB containers using GAN-based steganographic encodings, where recovery depends on a physics-informed pathway.

FocusContainer fidelity, hidden representation recovery, and PSF-conditioned restoration.
ToolsHiding networks, reveal networks, generative restoration, steganalysis metrics.
StatusSubmitted 2024
Steganography GANs PSF-Keyed Recovery PyTorch
System 03 · Privacy Stress Test

Reconstruction-based privacy analysis

Using GANs, Transformers, and physics-guided reconstruction models to evaluate how much visual information can be recovered from encoded measurements.

FocusMeasure leakage by testing how well adversarial models recover visual content.
ToolsGANs, Swin/Transformer models, FNO modules, perceptual and fidelity metrics.
GANs Transformers Privacy Leakage
System 04 · High-Energy Physics

Real-time anomaly detection using event-by-event SVD

Low-latency inference for streaming detector data with trigger-like constraints. Online feature extraction and anomaly detection for rare-event discovery with scaled data cleaning pipelines.

FocusStreaming ML, dimensionality reduction, trigger systems, GPU/FPGA acceleration.
ToolsSVD, Spark/Hadoop/Elasticsearch pipelines for dimensionality reduction.
SVD FPGA Dimensionality Reduction Streaming ML
